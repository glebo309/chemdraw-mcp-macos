CHEMDRAW / MCP

A quick guide to opening setup.

This experimental build is not notarized by Apple, so macOS may block its first launch. Only continue if you downloaded it from the official project releases and trust the source.

First: Double-click ChemDraw MCP in the disk image.

If setup opens, skip the steps below. If macOS blocks it, dismiss the warning with Done or OK. Do not choose Move to Trash if you intend to continue.

If macOS blocks the app

1Open settings

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security. Scroll down to the Security section.

2Allow this app

Find the entry for ChemDraw MCP, then click Open Anyway.

3Confirm opening

In the next dialog, click Open Anyway or Open, depending on macOS. Authenticate if asked.

Screenshot, not clickable controls. The confirmation dialog (3) appears after you click Open Anyway (2). Labels vary by macOS version. No Open Anyway entry? Try launching the app once more, then return here. A managed Mac may require your IT administrator.

Updating an existing installation? No uninstall needed.

Save your work and quit ChemDraw and connected assistants first. Open this new setup app and follow its steps. It refreshes the existing add-in; do not add a second copy. After the connection test, choose Finish setup and restart your assistants. Update guide

Then follow the setup window.

Select your licensed ChemDraw app and assistants. Follow the add-in instructions, test the connection, and finish. Restart your selected assistants afterwards.

A second permission is different.

During setup, macOS may ask to let ChemDraw MCP control ChemDraw. Choose Allow to connect them. This is Automation permission, separate from opening the installer.

This guide works offline. No Terminal commands or global security changes are needed. This warning does not prove the app is safe; do not use these steps for an alert saying malware was detected or the app will damage your computer. Apple’s opening-app guidance · Full setup guide
